# 🏦 Amzad Consultancy Hub & Advisory

**Amzad Consultancy Hub & Advisory** (Founded by **Md Amzad Khan**) is a production-grade, highly responsive financial technology and advisory application built with React, TypeScript, Vite, and Tailwind CSS. It empowers borrowers, homebuyers, and business owners with instant calculation suites, CIBIL credit score estimations, loan eligibility evaluations, and direct consultation booking.

---

## 🌟 Key Features & Financial Tools

### 🧮 1. Financial Calculators Suite
- **Tool #1: Professional EMI Calculator**: Interactive loan amount, interest rate, and tenure sliders with full amortization schedule breakdown and interactive chart previews.
- **Tool #2: Loan Eligibility Calculator**: Evaluates maximum loan eligibility based on gross monthly income, existing EMIs, FOIR standards, and age constraints across major banks.
- **Tool #3: CIBIL Score Estimator**: Algorithmic credit score estimator (300–900 range) factoring in payment history, credit card utilization %, hard enquiries, and active credit mix with instant SVG gauge visualization and tailored score improvement tips.
